# Scientific and editorial review: 20 October 2026

This review checked the authored guides against the pinned model, implementation, saved results and cited literature. Source retrieval was completed on 21 October UTC, during the review dated 20 October in the user’s local date. The original model, results and search logs are unchanged.

## Corrections and clarifications

- Corrected the distinction between biomass assembly and drainage. In this snapshot, `r_4041` combines biomass requirements and `r_2111` drains the biomass metabolite as the growth objective. Describing `r_2111` itself as the component-assembly reaction was imprecise.
- Defined flux units as millimoles per gram dry cell weight per hour and distinguished them from concentrations; the normalized biomass drain uses reciprocal hours.
- Made the theoretical 99% growth-floor explanation consistent with the implemented numerical slack. A 100% target is not exact equality, and a growth fraction is not a confidence level.
- Standardized author–year references, complete journal metadata and source roles. The publication’s model counts are kept distinct from the later v9.1.1 snapshot counts.
- Explained ordinary FVA as feasible per-reaction ranges, not simultaneous endpoint combinations, biological variability or calibrated uncertainty. A large-bound flag does not prove a thermodynamically infeasible cycle.
- Retained explicit limits around maintenance, the unmodified medium, the no-glucose diagnostic, oxygen-only changes, the Crabtree effect and comparisons with enzyme-constrained or dynamic models.
